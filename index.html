How to play
  • Joystick to walk. JUMP to jump.
  • PUSH! tap for a quick shove. Hold, then let go for a super push that sends people flying.
  • JUMP, then PUSH in the air = flying dropkick. Huge hit, but you land on your face.
  • GRAB (hold) grabs a strap, a door pole or a person. Holding a person + PUSH = throw. Your grip (green bar) runs out!
  • Out of the train for about a second = OUT. Hanging on the door pole is allowed, until a trackside pole comes.
  • Clutch save: flying out of the door? Hit GRAB near the pole to catch it. SAVED!
  • 🍔 Vada pav = super strength. ☕ Cutting chai = speed. 🎫 Ticket = safe when the TC comes.
  • Curves throw everyone sideways, brakes throw everyone forward. Stations bring crowds. Dadar, Andheri, Borivali: full rush.
Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space jump, J push (hold for power), K hold to grab.
Valid for one journey only · No refund for lost chappals · Do not hang from the door
