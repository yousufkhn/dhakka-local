DHAKKA LOCAL

Peak hour. Ek dabba. Sab andar ghusna chahte hain.
Push everyone out of the Mumbai local. Last one inside wins.

Kaise khelna hai (how to play)
  • ◀ ▶ walk. KOODO jump.
  • DHAKKA! shoves whoever is in front of you. Aim them at a door.
  • PAKDO (hold) grabs a strap, the door pole, or another person. Hold on during curves and brakes!
  • Out of the train for more than a second = BAHAR. Hanging on the door pole is allowed. Watch out for the khamba.
  • Station pe bheed aati hai. Dadar, Andheri, Borivali: full rush.
Keyboard: A/D or arrows to move, W/Space jump, J dhakka, K hold to pakdo.
